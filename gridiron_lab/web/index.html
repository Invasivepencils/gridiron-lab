04 / THE MATH BEHIND THE MODEL

Probability grounded
in linear algebra.

Each game is a row in a design matrix: +1 for the home team, −1 for its opponent, and a home-field term. A proper prior regularizes team contrasts and resolves their shared-offset ambiguity.

Conjugate Bayesian estimation

Posterior precision combines prior precision with XᵀX. Cholesky factorization solves for coefficient estimates and joint covariance. A Gamma posterior over noise precision yields a Student-t predictive distribution.

Simulation with explicit assumptions

Joint coefficient draws preserve correlations between team strengths. Each simulation samples game noise, player absence, and assumed replacement effects. The model currently assumes independent absences and does not learn player impacts.

Research status

This model has mathematical and leakage tests, but no demonstrated real-season forecasting edge. Historical ratings may already reflect injuries, so extra injury effects can double count. Binary scoring excludes ties; market settlement rules require separate treatment.
